Copy here the whole contents of the data1 folder of your Hexen II
installation (GOG version): pak0.pak, pak1.pak, progs.dat, progs2.dat,
strings.txt, hexen.rc and the rest.

Optional music: copy the music folder of the installation (Track02.ogg ...)
into this folder too, so that it becomes data1/music.

Upper or lower case names do not matter: the game renames them on start.
